ቃል (Qal) Keyboard: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24 · Developer: AbrhaBrothers · Contact: gsharewz@gmail.com

ቃል (“Qal”) is an Amharic and English keyboard for Android and iOS. Because a keyboard can see what you type, we built Qal so that everything stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Qal does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. The keyboard contains no networking code.

What is stored on your device

  • Learned words. To improve suggestions, Qal counts words you type and the word that came before them. This is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never learned from password fields or from fields that ask keyboards not to learn (“incognito” fields).
  • Settings and saved phrases. Your theme, toggles and the phrases you save are stored locally and shared only between the Qal app and the Qal keyboard on the same device.
  • Drafts. Text in the Qal app’s writing screen is saved locally so you don’t lose it.

You can erase learned words at any time in Settings → Your data → Reset. Uninstalling Qal removes all of the above.

Permissions

  • Input method (Android) / Keyboard extension (iOS). Required for Qal to work as a keyboard.
  • “Allow Full Access” (iOS, optional). Lets the keyboard read your theme and phrases from the Qal app and use haptic feedback. Qal does not use it to send data anywhere.

Android shows a standard warning that any keyboard “may be able to collect all the text you type”. This warning appears for every keyboard. Qal does not collect your text.

Children

Qal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
